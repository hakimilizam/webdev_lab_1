# Singapore modest-wear market analysis

Checked 2026-10-18. Target: Meta ads to a WooCommerce store, Singapore, mainly Malay-Muslim women. Hari Raya Puasa 2027 is 10 March 2027. Buying window is roughly Dec 2026 to Feb 2027.

- Claude in Chrome was not available in this session. The container's headless browser could not reach TikTok: the network policy returned 403 for `www.tiktok.com`. Facebook, Shopee, Lazada, Google and the competitor sites were blocked the same way. WebFetch could not resolve these hosts either.

- MY means the Malaysia storefront. SG means the Singapore storefront. RM prices are not converted.

## 2. Shortlist: top 5 to test in Singapore

The ranking uses the evidence available here. That evidence is thin: TikTok Shop sold counts from Malaysia, a few Singapore listings, and Singapore brand prices from 2025–2026 shopping guides. No video view counts were available. Treat the ranking as a starting hypothesis to check against the Ad Library and TikTok before buying stock.

Raya 2027 colour signals disagree. SHALOKA pushes soft yellow and olive cherry ([discover page](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/baju-raya)). AL SARA's ranking puts sage green, dusty blue and olive green ahead of butter yellow, which it ranks 10th ([blossom2u.com](https://blossom2u.com/10-hot-trending-colours-for-baju-raya-2027-in-malaysia-the-colours-to-watch/)). OM3RA.KL is polling its followers on 2027 colours ([video](https://www.tiktok.com/@om3ra/video/7634777887165517063)). Spread stock across colours.

### 1. Kebaya moden, ironless (mini or labuh)

- Demand: five TikTok Shop MY kebaya listings show 549 to 1.2K sold each. BUTIK MARIA mini kebaya: 1.2K and 674. Hagia Sophia Saloma: 859. Carlanisa songket: 620. LYC Khalish: 549. In 2024, Malay Mail reported tailors naming kebaya as a top Raya pick ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2024/04/10/as-malaysia-guns-for-cultural-heritage-bid-tailors-say-kebaya-back-in-vogue-for-2024s-raya/124317)).
- SG price points: S$23.90–28.50 for a jumbo brukat tunic on Shopee SG. Kebaya from S$42 at Bayu Somerset ([TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/where-to-buy-baju-raya-johor-bahru/)). Kebaya from S$75 at Mozaic SG ([mozaicsg.com](https://mozaicsg.com/)).
- SG availability: limited in what I could see. A search of TikTok Shop SG returned no kebaya product pages. Shopee SG showed one tunic and kurung-kebaya listings at about S$46–48. This may reflect index coverage, not the real market.
- Competition: medium, on the evidence seen.
- Non-Muslim demand: kebaya is also ethnic attire for the Peranakan community (per the search summary of the Malay Mail result above and [EPFL Graph](https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/en/concept/2792633)). No sales data.
- Supply: Malaysia. All five listings are MY TikTok Shop sellers. BUTIK MARIA's TikTok handle is @butikmariajohor. Indonesia is a second option: Tanah Abang wholesalers sell "kebaya kurung Malaysia" styles, e.g. Syahid Enterprise ([shop page](https://tanahabang.online/shops/Syahid-Enterprise), [video](https://www.tiktok.com/@tanahabang.online/video/7502743433354759429)). One TikTok page claims a 3-piece wholesale minimum at a Tanah Abang shop ([discover page](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/grosir-baju-kurung-tanah-abang)).

### 2. Kurung moden family set (sedondon)

- Demand: EL'RINAA set sedondon (NELOV's) shows 1.6K sold, 4.8 from 357 reviews. It was the highest kurung count seen. Singapore media run yearly guides to matching family sets ([TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/baju-kurung-online-singapore/), [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/style-kids-hari-raya-clothes-where-to-buy/), [HoneyKids](https://honeykidsasia.com/hari-raya-fashion-for-kids/)).
- SG price points: kids from S$59 and adults from S$109–149 at local brands. Simply Rays goes up to S$189. Higher order value per family than a single kurung.
- SG availability: many local brands sell sets.
- Competition: high.
- Main risk: several sizes per order means more exchanges.
- Supply: Malaysia. MY TikTok Shop sellers (NELOV's; Aizara Collection's family set shows 72 sold). Kenanga Wholesale City in KL has baju kurung on levels 3 and 4. Blogs report minimums of 3–6 or 12 pieces; those reports are old ([KWC review](https://theperpetualsaturday.com/kwc-fashion-mall/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenanga_Wholesale_City)). Dropship: Shashalista lists international shipping to Singapore only, at DHL rates ([shashalista.com](https://www.shashalista.com/pasha)). Amar Amran advertises 10–20% dropship commission ([amaramran.my](https://www.amaramran.my/pages/join-our-dropship)).

### 3. Cotton loose-cut kurung

- Demand: Bayu Somerset Spring Kurung (100% cotton) shows 281 sold, 5.0 rating. OM3RA.KL, a cotton kurung seller, has about 114.7K TikTok followers. On TikTok Shop SG, MIG's polyester kurung shows 263 sold, 4.9 from 63 reviews; reviews mention cooling fabric. Malay Mail's 2025 Raya trend piece headlined breathable fabrics ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/malaysia/2025/04/01/luxe-tones-classic-cuts-and-breathable-fabrics-whats-trending-for-raya-2025/170655)).
- SG price points: from S$42 (Bayu Somerset), S$45.90 (Shopee SG kurung moden clearance), from S$59 (Katt & Co).
- SG availability: yes. MIG sells on TikTok Shop SG. Bayu Somerset ships to Singapore from RM25.
- Competition: high on general kurung. I found no data on how many sellers push cotton specifically.
- Can be worn after Raya, so the selling season is longer than for festive pieces.
- Supply: Malaysia. Bayu Somerset is in Johor Bahru, so stock can come by land. Note that Bayu Somerset already sells direct to Singapore buyers. OM3RA.KL is in KL.

### 4. Plain como crepe ironless jubah (nursing-friendly)

- Demand: Zilofa's como crepe jubah shows 4.5K sold, 4.7 from 731 reviews. It was the highest count seen in any category. This is everyday wear, so it does not depend on Raya.
- SG price points: low. Cross-border abayas on TikTok Shop SG sell at S$16.81–62.62. A chiffon abaya on Shopee SG sells at S$7.15–26.07 with 360 sold.
- SG availability: many listings.
- Competition: high, driven by price.
- Supply: Malaysia ("Ready Stock Malaysia" in the Zilofa title). The cheap SG listings carry "cross-border" and "Middle East" keywords, which suggests China. Their origin is not stated. Alibaba manufacturers list 30–50 piece minimums ([Alibaba](https://www.alibaba.com/showroom/baju-kurung-malaysia-dropshipping.html)).

### 5. Kaftan or kaftan-batik set

- Demand: a kaftan-batik set on TikTok Shop SG shows 106 reviews at S$45.00. Jubah V / Abaya V shows 251 reviews. A creator promoted a kaftan at S$18.87 (from S$34.31).
- SG price points: S$16.81–45.00 on TikTok Shop SG.
- SG availability: yes.
- Competition: high, at a low price per item.
- Non-Muslim demand: kaftans are also sold as resort or house wear (Naiise resort kaftan at S$88; a Vitri Dolly kaftan listed as sleepwear). No sales data.
- Supply: origin not stated on the listings.

Add-on, not a core product: cotton jersey tudung. FindNiche's 2025 SG list shows one at S$7.90 with 565 orders in 7 days ([FindNiche](https://findniche.com/tiktok/trending-muslim-fashion-products-sg); 2025 data, rough guide). Use it as a bundle item to raise order value.

Near misses: satin kurung (Zehraa, 520 sold MY), kurung moden 15D (Sheila Apparel, 817 sold MY), embroidered jubah with free shawl (UniversalHub.MY, 795 sold MY).

## 6. Risks

Shipping from Malaysia to Singapore:

- Per-parcel express under 1kg: RM30–50, 2–5 working days. This is Ninja Van's estimate ([Ninja Van](https://blog.ninjavan.co/en-my/xb-b2c-how-to-ship-from-malaysia-to-singapore-cheapest-options-costs-and-restrictions/)). EMS through EasyParcel from RM72.50 (promo rate), 3 working days ([EasyParcel](https://blog.easyparcel.com/my/?p=91021)). Bayu Somerset charges from RM25.
- On a S$40–60 item, per-order cross-border shipping takes a large share of the price. Holding stock in Singapore avoids this but needs an upfront buy.
- GST: since 1 January 2023, low-value goods (S$400 or less) imported by air or post fall under the overseas vendor registration regime. Goods above S$400 pay GST at import. The change did not affect sea and land imports ([Singapore Customs](https://www.customs.gov.sg/businesses/customs-schemes-licences-framework/iras-schemes/overseas-vendor-registration-regime/)). Confirm how your stock shipments are taxed with Singapore Customs or IRAS.

Sizing:

- Sizes differ by seller. OM3RA.KL gives height and weight per size. Singapore brands go up to 4XL or 5XL (Simply Rays, Adrianna Yariqa), and Lubna is sold as size-inclusive.
- Publish garment measurements in cm and the model's height on every product page.

Returns:

- A cross-border return costs a second shipment. Some marketplace sellers refuse size changes; the kaftan-batik set listing says so.
- Late delivery before Raya makes the item worth less to the buyer and raises refund risk. Publish order cut-off dates.
- Colour complaints happen; one Jubah V reviewer said the white was off-white. Shoot in daylight.

Small audience:

- Malays were 13.5% of the resident population in the 2020 census ([Karyawan](https://karyawan.sg/2020-census-a-community-perspective)). Muslims were 15.6% of residents aged 15 and over (539,251) ([Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2021/06/singapore-residents-no-religion//amp), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Singapore)). The 2025 General Household Survey puts Muslims at 15% and Malays at 98.6% Muslim ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/singapore/2026/06/30/nearly-one-in-four-singapore-residents-now-have-no-religion-new-govt-data-reveals/225797)).
- Meta audience size was not checked; it needs an Ads Manager login. With a small audience, the same people see the ads often. Plan several creatives.
- Non-Muslim demand: no data found. The likely crossover items are kebaya (Peranakan wear) and kaftan (resort and house wear). Lazada SG sells kids' baju kurung for Racial Harmony Day ([Lazada SG](https://www.lazada.sg/tag/baju-kurung-kids/)). That is a separate, kids-only occasion.

Competition and price pressure:

- TikTok Shop Singapore reportedly has about 80,000 sellers ([Retail News Asia](https://retailnews.asia/tiktok-shop-singapore-seller-base-reaches-80000-as-video-sales-double)). Cross-border abayas sell from S$16.81.

Content:

- In 2025, The Playmate Stores was called out on TikTok for Quranic-print swimwear and pulled its Muslim-themed products ([SAYS](https://says.com/my/news/online-store-takes-down-swimwear-with-quranic-verses-after-being-called-out-on-tiktok)). Avoid prints with religious text.
